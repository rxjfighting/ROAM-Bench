Independent checks separate outcome correctness from task-required observations and interactions. Stronger checks add task-specific geometric, coverage, or placement corroboration.

A

Outcome correctness

Is the answer correct, or does the final state meet the goal?

N

Required observations & actions

Did the agent observe the required targets and complete the prescribed search and interactions?

S

Stronger corroboration

Do the required checks pass, together with additional task-specific checks?

T

Task success

A correct outcome and complete task requirements: T = A ∧ N.

Scoring rule

Task success requires both.

The outcome must be correct and the task requirements complete.

TTask success

S combines the required checks with additional geometry, coverage, or placement checks. Passing S does not guarantee a correct answer.
